# Week 4 -- UML Activity and Collaboration Diagrams

## Objective

The objective of this week was to model the behaviour and interaction of
the proposed system using UML. Based on the project requirements and
workflows identified in earlier weeks, I created the **Activity Diagram
and Collaboration Diagram**.

## Activities Completed

-   Reviewed the functional requirements and module structure prepared
    during the previous weeks.
-   Identified important workflows within the proposed platform.
-   Studied how behavioural UML models can represent the flow of a
    system.
-   Identified the activities involved in a system operation.
-   Created the Activity Diagram for the project.
-   Identified the objects/components participating in an interaction.
-   Created the Collaboration Diagram to represent communication and
    message flow between the participating objects.
-   Reviewed both diagrams against the previously identified
    requirements.
-   Made sure the diagrams represented the intended workflow and
    interactions of the system.

## Activity Diagram

The Activity Diagram was used to represent the flow of activities within
the system.

While preparing the diagram, I considered:

-   Initial and final states.
-   Sequence of activities.
-   User actions.
-   System responses.
-   Decision points and alternative flows.
-   Movement from one activity to another.

The diagram provides a behavioural view of how a selected operation
progresses through different steps.

## Collaboration Diagram

The Collaboration Diagram was prepared to represent how different
objects/components communicate while performing a functionality.

The diagram focuses on:

-   Objects involved in the operation.
-   Relationships between participating objects.
-   Messages exchanged between objects.
-   The order of interactions.
-   Responsibility of different objects during the operation.

This helped in understanding the interaction aspect of the proposed
system in addition to its activity flow.

## UML and Requirements Modelling

The work was connected with the Software Engineering syllabus topic of
**Requirements Modelling through UML**, including scenario-based and
behavioural modelling.

The diagrams were developed from the requirements and modules identified
in earlier weeks, allowing the requirements to be represented visually
rather than only through textual descriptions.

## My Contribution

My main contribution during this week was the creation of the **Activity
Diagram and Collaboration Diagram**.

I analyzed the planned functionality, converted the relevant workflows
into UML representations, and reviewed the diagrams to ensure

## Outcome

The Activity Diagram and Collaboration Diagram were completed. The
Activity Diagram provided a clear representation of the workflow, while
the Collaboration Diagram showed how participating objects/components
interact during the selected functionality.

These diagrams provide supporting design documentation for the
subsequent implementation and testing stages of the project.
